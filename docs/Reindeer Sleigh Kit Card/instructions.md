# Assembly Instructions for instructions

Welcome to the assembly instructions for your new "Reindeer Sleigh Kit Card" on MakerWorld project! Follow these steps 
carefully to ensure a successful build.

---

## Step 00: Gather Your Materials

1.  Begin by gathering all the components from your kit.
    ![Gather the sprues cards](Step%2000%20-%20Gather%20the%20sprues%20cards.jpg)

---

## Step 01: Assemble Sleigh Seats and Sides

1.  Locate the seat pieces and the main sleigh side panels.
    ![Gather seats and sleigh side](Step%2001a%20-%20Gather%20seats%20and%20sleigh%20side.jpg)
2.  Carefully attach each seat piece to one of the sleigh side panels, ensuring they fit securely.
    ![Attach Seat to Sleigh Side](Step%2001b%20-%20Attach%20Seat%20to%20Sleigh%20Side.jpg)

---

## Step 03: Construct the Sleigh Body

1.  Take the sleigh bottom piece and slot it into one of the assembled sleigh sides. The bottom piece should be somewhat flexible; gently bend it as needed to properly align and fit into the slots.
    ![Slot sleigh bottom into the side](Step%2003a%20-%20Slot%20sleigh%20bottom%20into%20the%20side%20-%20It%20should%20be%20relatively%20flexible.%20Bend%20it%20as%20needed%20to%20get%20it%20to%20fit.jpg)
2.  Once the bottom piece is partially secured, attach the other sleigh side panel, enclosing the bottom piece and forming the main body of the sleigh.
    ![Put other side onto the sleigh](Step%2003b%20-%20Put%20other%20side%20onto%20the%20sleigh.jpg)

---

## Step 04: Attach Sleigh Runners

1.  Carefully clip the sleigh runners into the designated slots on the ornamental border pieces. Ensure they are firmly in place.
    ![Clip in sleigh runners into the ornamental border](Step%2005a%20-%20Attach%20ornamental%20border%20to%20the%20sides%20of%20the%20sleigh.jpg)

---

## Step 05: Attach Ornamental Borders

1.  Attach the first ornamental border, now with the runners attached, to one of the sides of the sleigh. Align the tabs and slots for a secure fit.
    ![Attach ornamental border to the sides of the sleigh](Step%2005a%20-%20Attach%20ornamental%20border%20to%20the%20sides%20of%20the%20sleigh.jpg)
2.  Repeat the process for the second ornamental border, attaching it to the other side of the sleigh.
    ![Attach ornamental border to the sides of the sleigh](Step%2005b%20-%20Attach%20ornamental%20border%20to%20the%20sides%20of%20the%20sleigh.jpg)

---

## Step 06: Prepare Reindeer Ornaments

1.  Gather the two individual reindeer ornaments that will decorate your sleigh.
    ![Gather the two reindeer ornaments](Step%2006a%20-%20Gather%20the%20two%20reindeer%20ornaments.jpg)
2.  Press the small reindeer firmly into the pre-cut holes located on the sides of the sleigh, as indicated in the image.
    ![Press the small reindeer into the holes on the sides](Step%2006b%20-%20Press%20the%20small%20reindeer%20into%20the%20holes%20on%20the%20sides.jpg)

---

## Step 07: Assemble the Pull Bar and Collar

1.  Identify the components for the pull bar and the reindeer collar.
    ![Pieces for the pull bar and collar](Step%2007a%20-%20Pieces%20for%20the%20pull%20bar%20and%20collar.jpg)
2.  Assemble the collar piece as shown, creating the complete collar structure.
    ![Step 07b - Assembled Collar with Reindeer.jpg](Step%2007b%20-%20Assembled%20Collar%20with%20Reindeer.jpg)

---

## Step 08: Attach Reindeer and Pull Bar to Sleigh

1.  Ensure your reindeer are positioned and ready to be attached to the sleigh's harness points.
    ![Step 8a - Reindeer ready to attach to sleigh.jpg](Step%208a%20-%20Reindeer%20ready%20to%20attach%20to%20sleigh.jpg)
2.  Attach the assembled pull bar to the sleigh runner, ensuring a secure connection. Refer to the close-up image for proper alignment.
    ![Step 08b - Close-up of pull bar attached to sleigh runner.jpg](Step%2008b%20-%20Close-up%20of%20pull%20bar%20attached%20to%20sleigh%20runner.jpg)

---

## Step 09: Finished!

Congratulations! Your "instructions" project is now complete.
![Step 09 - Finished!.jpg](Step%2009%20-%20Finished%21.jpg)
